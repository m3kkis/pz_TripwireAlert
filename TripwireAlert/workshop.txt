title=Tripwire Alerts!
description=Place a bell tripwire and when something walks through, a bell rings and alerts you of incoming danger but be careful, nearby zombies can hear it too.
description=
description=Crafting:
description=Tripwire Kit (Trapping 1): 1 Wire, 1 Alarm Clock, 2 Wooden Sticks or Tree Branches. No tools.
description=
description=How it works:
description=1.Right-click the kit and choose Place.
description=2.Click a start tile, then an end tile (1 to 3 tiles, same floor, no walls in the way).
description=3.The wire is armed when you place it.
description=4.Zombies, animals, vehicles and even players can snap it.
description=
description=After the wire snaps: Repair with 1 Wire, or spend 1 Wire to pick up the kit. Arm and disarm from the world menu.
description=
description=WARNING: You get a grace period before you can trigger the trip wire. If you want to pass without breaking the line you need to crouch/stealth.
description=
description=Notes:
description=Build 42.20+ Works in singleplayer, multiplayer is not tested yet but should work
